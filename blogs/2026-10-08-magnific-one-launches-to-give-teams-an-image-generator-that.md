Generative AI image generation has come a long way from the early days of DALL-E and Midjourney in 2021-2022. But even with the most advanced models now supporting generated text and complex infographics, perceptive human beings can still detect and recoil from the "AI look" that many of these generated images exhibit — things like overly detailed textures, synthetic sheen, foods and products that appear far too numerous and voluptuous compared to their real world counterparts.
