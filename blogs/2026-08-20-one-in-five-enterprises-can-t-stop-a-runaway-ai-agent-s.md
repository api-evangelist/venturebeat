Enterprise AI teams have stopped betting on a single orchestration platform. The median enterprise now runs three at once — not by accident, but because none of them fully trusts a single vendor to run the show, according to VB Pulse data . This is not just to avoid vendor lock-in and retain flexibility (although that’s a big part of it).
